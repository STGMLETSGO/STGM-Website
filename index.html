Sales and Trading Group at McIntire

The Sales & Trading Group at McIntire begins with a recruitment process designed to identify students who demonstrate intellectual curiosity, professionalism, and initiative. Members then complete a comprehensive training curriculum featuring weekly educational sessions on market fundamentals, macroeconomics, equities, fixed income, derivatives, commodities, valuation, and recruiting preparation. The organization provides students with a structured pathway to careers in sales & trading, asset management, equity research, and other capital markets roles.

This years roadmap

Curriculum

Technical Training Sessions

A structured curriculum of trainings builds the technical and industry knowledge members need to walk into recruiting, and eventually a desk, prepared.

Immersion

New York Trip

Members travel to New York to meet with executives and tour financial firms where they gather insight into each firm’s culture and day-to-day operations. The trip also includes a visit to the New York Stock Exchange, allowing members to experience the center of the global capital markets firsthand.

Speaker Series

Industry Speaker Forums

Seasoned professionals join us throughout the year to share insight into industry roles, career paths, and how markets are actually trading beyond the textbook.

How to Join

The interview process

The interview process assumes some prior knowledge and experience, but we prioritize genuine curiosity about markets and the initiative to deepen that understanding.
